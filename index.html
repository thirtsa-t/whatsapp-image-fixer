Downloading isn't available in this view. Right-click the image (or long-press on a phone) and choose "Save image".

After downloading

  1. In the admin, open the article and replace the featured image with the downloaded file, then save.
  2. If the link was already shared before, add ?v=5 (or any new number) to the end when you post it, so WhatsApp loads the new image.
  3. Paste the link and wait until the image appears in the preview before you press send.
